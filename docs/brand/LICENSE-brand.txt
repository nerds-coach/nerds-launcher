NERDS brand assets

The NERDS name, the N mark and the N.E.R.D.S. lettering (every logo file in this directory, in
every color, size and format, and the renders made from them: the app icon in assets/app-icon/,
the tray icons and the installer art) belong to NERDS. They are not covered by the MIT OR
Apache-2.0 license of the code, and nothing in that license grants any right to them.

The vector files were redrawn from the PNG logo NERDS supplied (docs/brand/source/make_logo.py
records how), and the app tiles were drawn for NERDS (docs/brand/source/make_icons.py).

This repository is a modified version of ArtCraft Launcher. As ArtCraft's brand terms require of
forks, it carries none of the ArtCraft name, logo or "A" mark as branding; ArtCraft appears only
where the upstream project is credited (NOTICE, README.md, ATTRIBUTION.md).
